## About me

I am a product professional with a foundation in software and data engineering and an MBA journey at IIM Bangalore. My work sits at the intersection of **customer insight, product strategy, data, AI, and technical execution**.

I enjoy the complete product lifecycle: product discovery, customer research, pain-point identification, MVP definition, rapid prototyping, delivery, launch, adoption, and iteration. I am particularly interested in **Product Manager and AI Product Manager roles** where I can translate customer and business needs into clear product requirements while working closely with engineering, design, data, operations, and go-to-market teams.

## How I approach product work

### Discover and define

- Conduct customer research and user interviews to understand workflows, unmet needs, and underlying pain points.
- Translate insights into product vision, product strategy, business cases, and outcome-oriented roadmaps.
- Define PRDs, business requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, assumptions, and measurable success metrics.
- Prioritize opportunities by customer value, strategic alignment, business impact, evidence, risk, and engineering effort.

### Build and deliver

- Shape focused MVPs, manage scope and backlog priorities, and make technical and product trade-offs explicit.
- Partner cross-functionally with engineering, design, analytics, operations, and stakeholders throughout the end-to-end product lifecycle.
- Use APIs, SQL, cloud platforms, and rapid prototyping to validate ideas and communicate effectively with technical teams.
- Plan for product launch, go-to-market strategy, adoption, feedback loops, and continued growth—not just feature delivery.

### Measure and learn

- Define KPIs, north-star and guardrail metrics, instrumentation, and success criteria before launch.
- Use experimentation, qualitative feedback, and quantitative analysis to drive product decisions.
- Connect shipped work to value realization, user outcomes, and measurable business impact.
- Treat unsuccessful experiments and failure modes as structured learning inputs for the roadmap.
